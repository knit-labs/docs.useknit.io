# `PAYOUT_FAILED`

Delivered when Knit stops trying to send a payout, and no transfer for it was
made on chain. The payout status is `FAILED` and Knit does not retry it
automatically.

<Meta>
  <Chip label="Envelope" value="body wrapper" />
  <Chip label="Keys" value="camelCase" />
</Meta>

Delivered to your business webhook URL.

<Callout type="warning">
  `PAYOUT_FAILED` is **not** the end of the payout. The amount stays held in
  your API account. Knit support can still send the payout (you then get
  `PAYOUT_SUCCESSFUL`) or refund it (you then get `PAYOUT_CANCELLED` and
  `PAYOUT_REFUNDED`). Do not send the same payment again under a new
  `merchantReference` until the old payout is refunded.
</Callout>

## When it is sent

Knit sends this event only when it knows that nothing for the payout can be on
chain:

- the payout could not be submitted, or
- every automatic retry failed and Knit holds no transfer for it, or
- Knit stopped retrying the payout, and none of its attempts got as far as
  sending it.

A `FAILED` payout that still has automatic retries left produces no event. If
Knit cannot confirm that nothing was sent, it sends no event and its support
team checks the payout.

```json filename="PAYOUT_FAILED"
      "feeByToken": "0.500000000000000000",

      "status": "FAILED",

      "retryCount": 0,

```

<Fields>
  <Field name="payout.status" type="string">
    Always `FAILED`.
  </Field>
  <Field name="payout.transactionHash" type="string | null">
    Always `null`: nothing was sent.
  </Field>
  <Field name="payout.info" type="string">
    A short reason for the failure, for your support team. The wording
    differs between failures and can change, so do not parse it.
  </Field>
  <Field name="payout.nextRetryAt" type="string | null">
    Always `null`: Knit does not retry the payout automatically.
  </Field>
</Fields>

## Handling

<Steps>

### Verify and acknowledge

Check `X-Signature` against the raw body, then return `2xx` straight away. See
[Webhooks](/webhooks) for the verification snippet.

### Deduplicate on `merchantReference`

This event can arrive more than once. Later events for the same
`merchantReference` replace it: `PAYOUT_SUCCESSFUL` if the payout is sent, or
`PAYOUT_CANCELLED` and then `PAYOUT_REFUNDED` if it is refunded.

### Hold the payment

Mark the payout as failed in your ledger, but keep the payment open. Contact
Knit support to have it sent or refunded.

</Steps>
