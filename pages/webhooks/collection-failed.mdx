# `COLLECTION_FAILED`

Delivered when a collection cannot be completed. No funds have been credited to
your API account.

<Meta>
  <Chip label="Envelope" value="body wrapper" />
  <Chip label="Keys" value="snake_case" />
</Meta>

Delivered to the collection's `merchantCallbackUrl`, falling back to your
business webhook URL.

## Handling

Mark the payment attempt as failed on your side and, if the customer still
wants to pay, [create a new collection](/collections/create-a-collection) —
addresses are single-use and cannot be revived.

<Callout type="info">
  A collection that simply expires unpaid is a different situation: no funds
  moved and there is nothing to reconcile. Use `expires_at` to close out
  abandoned checkouts rather than waiting for an event.
</Callout>
